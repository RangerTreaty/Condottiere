Condottiere

Game Setup

Starting the Game

  1. Choose the first player randomly.
  2. Give the first player the Condottiere Token.
  3. Shuffle the 96-card deck.
  4. Deal 10 cards to each player.
  5. The player holding the Condottiere Token chooses the city where the first battle takes place.

Victory Conditions

  • 4–6 players: Control 4 cities in adjacent regions.
  • 3 players: Control 5 cities in adjacent regions.
  • 2 players: Control 6 cities in adjacent regions.

Cities must form one connected territory. The first player to meet their victory condition wins immediately.

Card Deck Composition

A standard poker deck setup using two 52-card decks plus Jokers. You only need the cards listed below (96 total):

Full Rules

Objective

Players are rival mercenary leaders fighting for control of cities. Battles are resolved by playing cards. Build a connected territory and control enough cities to win.

Playing a Battle

The Condottiere Token holder selects the city and plays first. Play proceeds clockwise. On your turn: play 1 card or Pass. Passing means you cannot play more cards during that battle.

Defending a City

If defending your own city, you may pass repeatedly without elimination until you play your first card. Afterwards, standard passing rules apply.

Army Strength & Special Cards

  • Scarecrow (8/9): Return one played Mercenary card back to owner's hand.
  • Drum (Jack): Doubles value of all your Mercenary cards.
  • Winter (Black Q): Every Mercenary card becomes worth 1 point.
  • Heroine (Red Q): Worth 10 points. Unaffected by Winter or Drum.
  • Surrender (Black K): Battle ends immediately. Strongest army at that moment wins.
  • Bishop (Joker): Battle ends immediately. Nobody wins the city.

End of Round & New Hands

Round ends when only one player has cards left or all players discard their hands. New deal: 10 cards + 2 cards for every city controlled.
